Deficiencies & Recommendations 2 deficiencies · 1 recommendation
1Relief valve discharge not piped to a visible pointOutstanding
Re-pipe the relief-valve discharge to a floor cone visible from the controller per NFPA 20 §4.20. Until corrected, relief flow cannot be visually confirmed during the weekly test. Ref. checklist item 4 · Contractor: Vipond Fire Protection
2Battery-charger AC-failure alarm inoperativeOutstanding
Service battery charger and verify the AC-failure supervisory contact annunciates at the controller. Ref. checklist item 8 · Contractor: Vipond Fire Protection
3Label fuel-tank shut-off valveRecommendation
Add a durable identification tag to the fuel-supply shut-off valve to aid operators during maintenance. Does not affect acceptance.
