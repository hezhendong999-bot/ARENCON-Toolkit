FRT Camera Orientation Test

Mirrors FRT's exact capture. Tells us the one transform that keeps every photo upright on this tablet.

  1. Tap Open Camera.
  2. Hold the tablet upright (portrait), point at anything with a clear top/bottom (a sign, a door), tap the shutter.
  3. Rotate the tablet 90° left (landscape), shoot the same thing. Then 90° right. Then upside-down.
  4. Tap Done. For each shot, tell me which image is upright — or just screenshot this page and send it.
