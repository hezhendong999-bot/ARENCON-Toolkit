ARENCON — Contour selection

No bounding box. The selection indicator traces the mark's own shape — a triangle gets a triangle, a squiggle gets a squiggle.
Tap a mark to select it (tap another to add to the group). Drag near it — anywhere in the grab zone, not on the ink — to move it. Drag on empty photo to rubber-band. Tap far away to deselect.
